3 · V diagnosis: lift-off height × dip depth

A V attempt is S=(0,0) → A=(w/2, depth) → E=(w, Δy). Positive y points down the screen. The result doesn't depend on where on the screen you start, apart from the insets.

4 · Arc tolerance on a straight swipe

Model the swipe as a circular arc with chord length L and sagitta s. Only the path's bounding box gets checked, so the arc becomes a triangle gesture once s > h. Diagonal swipes have no triangle variant, so their arc tolerance is unlimited.
